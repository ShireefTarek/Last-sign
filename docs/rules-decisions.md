## Decisions received

- Ordinary final lot changes are free; the declaration remains non-binding.
- Crowding counts real players targeting open lots; tied counts may qualify as both most and least crowded.
- Scandal is evaluated at round end; a temporary crossing reduced before then does not schedule a final round.
